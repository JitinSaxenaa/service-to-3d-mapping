# SERVICE BULLETIN SB-2019-04
## ADLOID GEARWORKS · applies to: GBX-450E all serials · issued 2019-04-02

Supersedes torque and quantity data in WM-GBX-450E rev.C and IPC-GBX-450E
rev.B where they conflict. **This bulletin is authoritative.**

### 1. Corrections to WM-GBX-450E rev.C

| Ref | Was | Is |
|---|---|---|
| Step 20-30 | "remove both mid-ring retaining bolts" | remove **three (3)** mid-ring bolts on the drive end. The 1998 text predates the third boss added at casting rev. E. |
| Step 30-10 note | "one bolt is fitted with a bonded washer" | bonded washer deleted 2007; all six rear-ring bolts identical. |
| Step 10-10 | "check the sight glass" | sight glass NOT FITTED on -E variants (see IPC GBX-SG-131, qty 0). Inspect drained oil instead. |

### 2. Corrections to IPC-GBX-450E rev.B

| PN | Field | Was | Is |
|---|---|---|---|
| GBX-HXB-122 | qty/assy | 16 | **18** (2 added at casting rev. E, front flange) |
| GBX-OS-124 | status | current | superseded -> **GBX-OS-124-B** (FKM lip). Physically interchangeable; same bore, same envelope. |
| GBX-SH-114 / GBX-OSH-115 | envelope | as printed | printed values are in **cm** (transcription); multiply by 10 for mm. |

### 3. Torque schedule (verified)

| Fastener | Torque |

| GBX-SCS-121 | 13 Nm |
